\textbf{B.1 Circular induction step.}
A student tries to prove $2^n\ge n+1$ for every integer $n\ge0$. In the
induction step, the student assumes $2^{k+1}\ge k+2$ and then declares
$P(k+1)$ proved.

Identify the error, state the correct induction hypothesis, and give a valid

\section*{C. Complete inductive proof}
\textbf{C.1 Binomial sum.}
You may use Pascal's identity and the endpoint identities
\[
  \binom{k+1}{j}=\binom{k}{j}+\binom{k}{j-1}
  \quad(1\le j\le k),
  \qquad
  \binom{n}{0}=\binom{n}{n}=1.
\]
Prove by mathematical induction that
\[
  \sum_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n}{j}=2^n
  \qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge0.
\]
Your proof must include the proposition $P(n)$, the base case, the induction
hypothesis, the complete next-case calculation (including reindexing), and the
conclusion.
