# COMP 2300 Solutions

These solution sets are available throughout the course. Use them to check your reasoning, compare proof structure, and revise work after making a serious attempt.

Each Core page includes the full practice question, a worked solution, and relevant readings/videos. The printable companions below collect definitions and worked examples; they are study references, not permitted materials during a supervised Core attempt unless an accommodation says otherwise.
